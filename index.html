Teleprompter Web App

A lightweight, privacy-focused, zero-dependency browser-based teleprompter built for smooth 60+ FPS scrolling and offline presentation workflows.

⚡ 60+ FPS Fluid Scroll
Sub-pixel precision engine using requestAnimationFrame and GPU hardware transforms.
🔒 100% Offline & Private
Zero tracking or external calls. Your scripts stay strictly in local storage.
🪞 Physical Glass Mirroring
Instant horizontal reflection mode designed for beam-splitter prompter glass rigs.
📱 PWA & Shortcut Driven
Installable to desktop or mobile with ergonomic keyboard and presentation controls.
